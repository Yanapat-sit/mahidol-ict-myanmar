What you gain at Mahidol ICT

Quality, cost and closeness to home – the three things Myanmar students and families ask us about most.

Finish what you started

If your studies were interrupted, this is a stable four-year path to the qualification you were already working towards – at one of Thailand's leading universities.

A cost your family can plan for

Clear yearly tuition of about 180,000 baht and lower living costs than many study destinations, so your family can budget all four years before you start.

Feel at home from week one

Thailand and Myanmar are neighbours with similar cultures, familiar food and shared Buddhist traditions. You'll join a growing community of Myanmar students studying in Thailand.

International graduates of Mahidol ICT have gone on to work at well-known technology and consulting firms. Your internationally recognised degree also travels home with you – for an ICT career in Myanmar or anywhere in ASEAN.
